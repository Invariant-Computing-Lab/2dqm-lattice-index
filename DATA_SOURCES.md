# Data and literature sources

## Locked empirical data
- Particle Data Group, *Review of Particle Physics* (2026), Particle Listings / Meson Summary Tables, listing cut-off 15 January 2026.
- Primary sequence: Upsilon(1S), Upsilon(2S), Upsilon(3S), Upsilon(4S).
- Secondary sensitivity sequences: chi_b1(1P-3P) and chi_b2(1P-3P); the PDG summary notes that the corresponding J assignments need confirmation.

The exact numerical values used by the analysis are preserved in `data/pdg2026_bottomonium_locked.csv` together with source-page notes.

## Mathematical/lattice background
The manuscript cites the original Nambu-Goto/Polyakov worldsheet literature, Atiyah-Singer index theory, Neuberger's overlap-fermion construction, and a recent lattice index study. These sources motivate the mathematical stress tests; they do not provide empirical support for 2DQM as a quark model.
